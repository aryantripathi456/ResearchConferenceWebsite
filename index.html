Our Foundation

We assemble sharp minds on one conviction — insight grows where disciplines collide.

04

About

A place built for the future of the intellect.

Since 1957, Jai Hind College has been a laboratory of ambition in Mumbai — faithful to one purpose: to research first, publish fast, and share sooner.

INSIGHT '27 inherits that purpose. It brings the college's 20 associated departments and research centres under one marquee — with every session designed so that a first-year scholar and a tenured professor leave with something more than they arrived with.

The conference is hosted under the academic patronage of the Research & Innovation Cell, with technical co-sponsorship from partner universities across Asia and Europe.

06

For Authors

Submit deliberately, reviewed fairly.

A transparent, double-stitched path from manuscript to publication.

ISubmission Guidelines

  • 01
    Abstract300–350 words with 3–5 keywords, stating the research question, method, and contribution. No author names during blind review.
  • 02
    Full length4,000–6,000 words in the official IEEE template; figures and tables mandatory for empirical panels.
  • 03
    OriginalityWork must be unpublished. A plagiarism ceiling of <15% (iThenticate) is strictly enforced.
  • 04
    LanguageEnglish, submitted in the portal irc2027.jhcollege.ac.in/submit under a legitimate institutional email.
  • 05
    Authors & feesUp to four co-authors. Early-bird fee holds until 10 January 2027; at least one author must register for inclusion.
Accepted abstracts are invited to expand into full papers for the proceedings; programme decisions are made only after the full paper is reviewed.

IIReview Process

1
Desk Screening

Editors verify format, scope, and completeness within 72 hours of submission.

2
Double-Blind Assignment

Two independent reviewers per paper — identity of both sides masks preserved.

3
Consolidated Verdict

Accept, revise (minor/major), or reject — communicated with actionable reports.

4
Revision & Author Response

Authors revise and submit a point-by-point rebuttal, if required.

5
Final Acceptance

Approve, index, publish in proceedings, and schedule the podium slot.
